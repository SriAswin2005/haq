# Where AI tools got it wrong

Format: `date | agent | what went wrong | how caught | fix`. Append only.

2026-11-05 | planning chat (another AI tool), caught by Claude | The corpus brief named the UP Urban Buildings (Regulation of Letting, Rent and Eviction) Act, 1972 as the UP rent law to collect, and the planning chat predicted "UP may have no specific rule on deposits". India Code lists the Uttar Pradesh Regulation of Urban Premises Tenancy Act, 2021 (UP Act 16 of 2021), which appears to replace the 1972 Act and has a security-deposit section (s. 11 per secondary sources). | Claude cross-checked the brief with a web search before any agent started; results included the India Code entry and several sources quoting the deposit section. | Corpus brief and architecture corrected to point at the 2021 Act as a lead. Antigravity must still confirm it from the official text, and the human must check the status.
