## Round-3 eval: pruned answers broke the frozen contract; slow tail from same-model retries
- **What happened:** Claim-level pruning returned `verifier.status = "pruned"`, which is not in the frozen TurnResult set (pass | fixed | failed). Codex's contract check failed 9 cases (T-004, T-005, T-008, T-014, T-023, T-024, T-025, T-031, T-033) whose answers were otherwise fine. Separately, p95 latency was 158–185 s: on a 5xx the client slept and retried the same overloaded model, and the Gemma 31B judge sometimes took very long.
- **Fix:** Pruned answers now return `status: "fixed"` with `pruned: true` (trace.pruned is kept). A 5xx/network error now skips straight to the next model with an escalating cooldown. Per-call timeout is 40 s (25 s for the Gemma judge). The server queues at most 4 waiting turns and answers "busy, try again in a minute" beyond that, instead of making people wait minutes.
- **Caught by:** Codex round-3 eval (tests/checks.py check_contract) and per-turn traces.

## Round 4 referrals: a weaker second office shown; a Hindi reply with Cyrillic letters
- **What happened:** In Codex's round-4 referral eval (T-041..T-055), the out-of-scope path showed a second office whenever it cleared 0.70, even when it was clearly a worse match: Child Helpline for a neglected elderly person (0.711 vs Elderline 0.794), CPGRAMS for an RTI request. In-scope answers also added the National Consumer Helpline beside the sector regulator (bank, airline, food safety). One Hindi reply contained "क्леम", with Cyrillic letters from the model.
- **Fix:** A second referral is shown only if its score is within 0.03 of the first (config `referral_second_margin`). The answer prompt now asks for the ONE body that most specifically handles the problem, with no general helpline when a sector body is in the sources. The verifier flags any block containing characters from an unrelated script (Cyrillic, Greek, CJK, Thai, Hangul, Kana), so that block is pruned or regenerated.
- **Caught by:** Codex round 4 (outputs/referrals.md) and my review of the raw replies.

## Round 4 full eval: English questions answered in Hindi; a misspelt government website
